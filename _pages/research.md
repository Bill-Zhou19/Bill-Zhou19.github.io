My work develops and integrates extrusion-based additive manufacturing (EAM) processes and translates them into scalable manufacturing solutions. Current directions include:

## 1) Solvent-free (Dry) Electrode Manufacturing
- Develop extrusion-based dry processing routes to fabricate electrodes without solvent-based slurry steps
- Study powder/binder/carbon network formation, densification, and adhesion mechanisms
- Engineer post-processing (e.g., rolling/calendering) for microstructure and performance tuning

## 2) EAM of Continuous CFRP Composites
- Improve interlayer bonding, fiber continuity, and structural integrity in printed CFRP parts
- Investigate process–structure–property relationships and failure mechanisms
- Explore design/printing strategies that balance stiffness, strength, and manufacturability

## 3) EAM of Tungsten-based Alloys
- Establish process windows and sintering/thermal profiles for high-density tungsten alloys
- Link printing parameters to densification, distortion, grain evolution, and mechanical behavior
- Build composition/process maps to guide manufacturable, high-density outcomes

## 4) RF Heating-enabled Processing
- Explore RF heating / EM–thermal coupling to accelerate or localize thermal processing
- Develop matching/coupling strategies to improve energy efficiency and process control
- Apply RF heating concepts to manufacturing-relevant materials and geometries
